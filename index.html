EXTRINSIC PARAMETERS

MEAN REPROJECTION ERROR

— px

OFFSET

A known synthetic scene, with a 640 × 400 camera and 460 px focal length. Error is computed from paired projections. “Align” restores the known reference transform; it does not run an optimization algorithm.

WHY IT MATTERS

Pose and calibration errors propagate into geometry, rendering, and evaluation. This is why inspecting the alignment is part of the reconstruction workflow.
